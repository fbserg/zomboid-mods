title=Dinner Together [B42] - A Meal With Good Company
description=[h1]The apocalypse is a lousy dinner guest.[/h1]
description=Eat with a friend. Feel a little better. Dinner Together adds a small comfort bonus when survivors share a meal nearby. Soup in a safehouse, sandwiches by the road, two apples after a supply run. It all counts.
description=
description=[h2]How to share a meal[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Stay within [b]four tiles[/b] of another living player, on the same floor, with a clear line of sight. Open doors count. Walls, closed doors and windows do not.
description=[*]Each person must finish at least [b]five hunger points[/b] of food in one normal eating action. Finish within [b]ten game minutes[/b] of each other and stay nearby.
description=[*]Both receive [b]5 less boredom and 3 less unhappiness[/b], plus the message [i]A meal with good company[/i].
description=[*]Each person gets the bonus at most [b]once per 24 game hours[/b]. A friend on cooldown can still keep you company.
description=[/list]
description=No plates, furniture, matching food, recipes or extra buttons. You do not need the kitchen in the artwork. A modest meal and another survivor will do.
description=
description=[h2]What counts[/h2]
description=Vanilla and modded food can qualify if they use the normal eating action. Tiny bites do not count. An interrupted action does not count, even if it consumed part of the food. Eating alone has no effect. Cooldowns belong to the username in multiplayer and the local player slot in single player, so a replacement character keeps that cooldown.
description=
description=[h2]Setup and multiplayer[/h2]
description=For Project Zomboid [b]Build 42.21[/b]. No required mods. Single player with local split-screen survivors, or multiplayer with other players. Install on the server and every client. Add [b]DinnerTogether[/b] to the server Mods list and this item to WorkshopItems.
description=The server decides the result and saves the cooldown across restarts. Verified with a dedicated server and two real clients. Network split-screen notifications currently target the first local player on each client.
description=
description=[h2]Adding, removing and compatibility[/h2]
description=Safe to add to an existing world. Adds no items, objects or loot. Removal leaves only inactive cooldown timestamps in world data. Another mod that replaces the normal eat-action completion without calling the previous function can block the effect.
description=The first gallery image is an illustration. Gameplay images use a disposable demonstration world with two connected players and real food actions. Food, time and starting stats are set for the demo. No special table or serving system is added.
description=
