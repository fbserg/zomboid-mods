title=Truck Repaint [B42] - KI5 Trucks & Buses
description=[h1]The world ended. Your truck still says Spiffo's.[/h1]
description=Repaint KI5's [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3110911330]'87 Ford B700/F700 Trucks[/url] into their plain factory colors with a paintbrush and ordinary paint buckets. Stop driving a rolling burger ad through the apocalypse. The zombies already know where you are. They do not need a coupon.
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Find a [b]paintbrush[/b] and some [b]paint buckets[/b]. Hardware stores have both, as usual.
description=[*]Right-click a supported truck and choose [b]Repaint Truck[/b], then a color.
description=[*]Your survivor walks to the driver door and paints. Walk away to stop.
description=[/olist]
description=The tooltip lists what you need. Red text means you are short. It does not judge your color choices. The zombie with the swatch card does.
description=
description=[h2]Colors and cost[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]F700 box truck:[/b] Blue and White, Red and Black, White, Yellow, Red and White
description=[*][b]F700 bank truck:[/b] Red and Black, Silver and Black
description=[*][b]F700 SWAT truck:[/b] Black
description=[*][b]B700 military, school and prison buses:[/b] Redneck Camo. The school bus finally looks like it skipped school.
description=[*][b]Oshkosh P19A military trucks:[/b] USMC Green, NATO Green, Desert Tan, Camo
description=[*][b]Oshkosh P19A fire truck:[/b] Red, Yellow, Red and White
description=[/list]
description=The Oshkosh colors need KI5's [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2566953935]'86 Oshkosh P19A[/url]. It is optional. Without it, those entries stay asleep.
description=A solid color uses [b]one full bucket[/b] of that color. A two-tone job uses [b]half a bucket of each[/b]. The brush survives. Empty buckets stay in your inventory, for sentimental reasons.
description=
description=[b]Paint covers a livery but never creates one.[/b] You can turn a Spiffo's, Gigamart, U-Store-It, Knox Bank, SWAT, sheriff or US Army truck into a plain one. You cannot paint a Spiffo's logo freehand. Nobody can.
description=
description=[b]Safe to add or remove.[/b] The mod adds no items and saves no data. Remove it and every truck keeps a normal KI5 paint job. A camo bus goes back to KI5's own skin.
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=For Project Zomboid [b]Build 42.21[/b]. The server and every player need Truck Repaint, the truck mod and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3171167894]that DAMN Library[/url]. Put [b]TruckRepaint[/b] after [b]87fordB700[/b] in the server's Mods list. The server checks and spends the paint, then every player sees the new color.
description=

Project Zomboid supplies the paint, brush, animation, sound and multiplayer sync. HDCP Immersive Vehicle Paint and Paint Your Ride inspired the idea. Their code is not used.
