description=[h1]This time I packed the can opener.[/h1]
description=Last supply run, you left Beth three tins of beans. She left you a note about the beans. This time you can wrap the tins and the opener together, put her name on the parcel, and tuck in a note of your own. She can keep it after lunch.

description=[h2]Leave somebody a parcel[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*]In the handcraft window, make a [b]Care Package[/b] with two sheets of paper and one use of twine.
description=[*]Hold it in either hand and fill its inventory tab. You get [b]10 units of capacity[/b]. A spare bandage, dry socks and the last decent book fit the occasion just as well.
description=[*]Right-click the parcel in your main inventory and choose [b]Seal Care Package[/b]. Fill in [b]To[/b] and [b]From[/b], then add a note if you like. Leave it on the kitchen floor or in the crate your friend always checks first.
description=[*]Pick it up and choose [b]Open Care Package[/b] from your main inventory. Hold the open parcel or set it down to unpack. [b]Read Parcel Note[/b] brings the message back after the contents are gone.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]What happens to the stuff inside?[/h2]
description=While the parcel is sealed, its contents stay out of the regular inventory and nearby crafting lists. A battered hammer keeps its condition, a loaded magazine keeps its rounds, and food keeps ageing. Bear that last one in mind before you leave a fish under someone’s bed.
description=Anyone who picks up the parcel can open it. Write Beth's name on the label and you're trusting your housemates to behave.
description=
