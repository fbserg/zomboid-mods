# Care Packages

Leave a friend more than a pile of loot. Pack a few things together, write their name and leave a note for when they find it.

## How to use it

1. In the handcraft window, make a **Care Package** from two sheets of paper and one use of twine.
2. Hold it in either hand. Fill its inventory tab with the things you want to send.
3. Right-click the parcel in your main inventory and choose **Seal Care Package**. Fill in **To** and **From** and add an optional note.
4. Drop the parcel or put it in a shared container. The recipient takes it into their main inventory and chooses **Open Care Package**.
5. Use **Read Parcel Note** to reread the note after opening. Hold the open parcel or set it on the ground to unpack it.

A parcel has capacity 10. Food ages as usual. Sealed contents are unavailable in the regular inventory and nearby crafting lists. Anyone who can pick up a parcel may open it.

## Installation

Care Packages 0.1.0 targets Project Zomboid Build 42.22. Enable the mod on a single-player save, or enable `CarePackages` on the server and each client. It has no dependencies. The parcel form uses a keyboard and mouse. Empty every parcel before removing the mod.
