title=Clean Misses [B42.22.2] - Firearm Range & RadArchery Bow Accuracy
description=[h1]The zombie flinched. Did you actually hit it?[/h1]
description=In Build 42.22, a zombie can bleed, flinch and keep every point of health. Apocalypse uses [b]Firearms Use Damage Chance: Zombies only[/b]. When the accuracy roll fails, the game can still register contact and play the hit reaction.
description=
description=[h2]What changes when you shoot[/h2]
description=Clean Misses uses the game's regular hit-chance mode. Fail the roll and the shot misses. Pass it and the game calculates damage as usual. Aiming skill, movement and weapon accuracy still matter. The fence behind the zombie appreciates your continued business.
description=
description=[h2]Firearm range (optional)[/h2]
description=Enable [b]Clean Misses → Gentler range penalty[/b] in sandbox settings and reload the world. The default is off. With it on, accuracy falls by two percentage points per tile outside the sight window. This also softens the penalty inside a scope’s minimum sight distance where the engine uses that calculation. Movement, skill, sights and the game's other penalties still matter. The shared calculation also applies to PvP.
description=
description=For a steady Hunting Rifle with base Aiming 0 and iron sights, the 42.22.2 damage roll at 20 tiles rises from 6% to 31%. At 10 tiles it stays at 51%. This is range tuning for the current build.
description=
description=[h2]RadArchery bows[/h2]
description=With RadArchery enabled, each registered bow uses a built-in sight range of 3 to 14 tiles. Projectile reach, arrow bonuses and Archery skill still come from RadArchery.
description=
description=At 15 tiles, the regular Compound Bow has a 6% chance to pass the damage roll in stock RadArchery, 71% with Clean Misses, and 74% with Gentler range penalty also enabled. The Heavy Compound Bow gives 6%, 76% and 79%. These are engine roll probabilities for a steady shot at a standing zombie with Archery 4, Aiming 0, a Pinpoint Carbon Arrow and no situational penalties. A player must still line up the shot.
description=
description=[h2]Do you need the mod?[/h2]
description=If you manage your own sandbox, set [b]Firearms Use Damage Chance[/b] to [b]Disabled[/b] to reproduce the default firearm feedback change alone. Enable Clean Misses if you want that choice applied automatically. It works through the game's combat setting and needs no other mods.
description=
description=[h2]Weapon packs and multiplayer[/h2]
description=Modded guns that use vanilla hit resolution inherit the change. A mod with its own projectile and damage system can make its own rolls. Disable any mod that sets minimum hit chance to 100 if you want normal accuracy.
description=
description=Enable [b]CleanMisses[/b] in your world's mod list. Server owners should add Workshop item [b]3842265515[/b] and enable the mod for the server and clients. If your server uses [b]All types of target[/b], failed rolls against players will also become misses.
description=
description=To remove it, disable Clean Misses and select your preferred sandbox mode. Tested against the Build 42.22.2 combat resolver.
description=
