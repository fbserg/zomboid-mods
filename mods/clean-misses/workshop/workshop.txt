title=Clean Misses [B42] - Gun Hit Feedback & RadArchery Bow Accuracy
description=[h1]The zombie flinched. Did you actually hit it?[/h1]
description=In Build 42.22, a zombie can bleed, flinch and keep every point of health. Apocalypse uses [b]Firearms Use Damage Chance: Zombies only[/b]. When the accuracy roll fails, the game can still register contact and play the hit reaction.
description=
description=[h2]What changes when you shoot[/h2]
description=Clean Misses uses the game's regular hit-chance mode. Fail the roll and the shot misses. Pass it and the game calculates damage as usual. Aiming skill, movement and weapon accuracy still matter. The fence behind the zombie appreciates your continued business.
description=
description=[h2]RadArchery bows[/h2]
description=With RadArchery enabled, each registered bow uses a built-in sight range of 3 to 14 tiles. Projectile reach, arrow bonuses and Archery skill still come from RadArchery. For vanilla guns, Clean Misses changes hit feedback only. Shots per kill at distance can still be high.
description=
description=[h2]Do you need the mod?[/h2]
description=If you manage your own sandbox, set [b]Firearms Use Damage Chance[/b] to [b]Disabled[/b] for the same vanilla firearm behavior. Enable Clean Misses if you want that choice applied automatically. It works through the game's combat setting and needs no other mods.
description=
description=[h2]Weapon packs and multiplayer[/h2]
description=Modded guns that use vanilla hit resolution inherit the change. A mod with its own projectile and damage system can make its own rolls. Disable any mod that sets minimum hit chance to 100 if you want normal accuracy.
description=
description=Enable [b]CleanMisses[/b] in your world's mod list. Server owners should add Workshop item [b]3842265515[/b] and enable the mod for the server and clients. If your server uses [b]All types of target[/b], failed rolls against players will also become misses.
description=
description=To remove it, disable Clean Misses and select your preferred sandbox mode. Tested against the Build 42.22.2 combat resolver.
description=
