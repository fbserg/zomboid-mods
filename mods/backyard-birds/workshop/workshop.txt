title=Backyard Birds [B42] - Wild Birds at the Bird Bath
description=[h1]The world ended. The cardinals did not get the memo.[/h1]
description=Wild Kentucky birds visit the vanilla [b]bird bath[/b]. They land one at a time, drink, splash, sing, and leave when you get too close. Stand still for a minute. It helps.
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Find or place a [b]Bird Bath[/b] outdoors. Plenty of yards already have one.
description=[*]Birds come in the [b]morning[/b] and the [b]evening[/b]. Midday is quiet. Night, rain and frozen water mean no birds.
description=[*]Hot days and fresh rain bring more of them.
description=[*]A visit lasts a short while, then the bath goes quiet again.
description=[*]Walk up and they scatter. [b]Sneak[/b] and you can get much closer. Run and all you see is tail feathers. Zombies scare them too, which is fair.
description=[*]A visit lifts boredom and unhappiness a little, at most once an hour.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Who comes by[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Northern Cardinal, Blue Jay and Carolina Chickadee, all year
description=[*]American Robin, March to October
description=[*]American Goldfinch, April to October
description=[*]Eastern Bluebird, rarely. You will know.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Safe to add or remove.[/b] The mod adds no items or tiles and saves nothing. The birds live on your screen, not in your save.
description=[b]Light.[/b] One small check every ten game minutes, skipped at night, and a few sprites while birds visit.
description=Birds are drawn on top of the world, so a wall in front of the bath does not hide them. The screenshots show three staged baths with visits at once. In play, visits come and go.
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=For Project Zomboid [b]Build 42.21[/b], single player or multiplayer. Install it on the server and for every player, and add [b]BackyardBirds[/b] to the server's Mods list. The server picks the visits, so everyone nearby sees the same birds. Each player scares off their own.
description=
