title=Grocery List [B42] - Errands for a Friend
description=[h1]Nobody remembers the batteries unless they are on the list.[/h1]
description=Grocery List adds a one-page notepad for errands. Write what you need, one item per line. Whoever carries the list sees each line crossed off as soon as they carry a matching item. Finish the list and feel a little better. Bring a friend's list back to them and you both do.
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Make a list.[/b] Craft a Grocery List from a sheet of paper, graph paper or a notepad (Miscellaneous). Right-click it and choose Write Note. Like any vanilla notebook, you need a pen or pencil to write.
description=[*][b]Write one item per line.[/b] Part of the in-game name is enough. [i]beans[/i] matches Canned Beans and [i]batteries[/i] matches Battery. "- milk" and "2 x rice" work too. Quantities are only notes. Lines under three letters and lines that start with ~ are ignored.
description=[*][b]Go shopping.[/b] Carry the list. Once a game minute, every line that matches something you carry, bags included, gets an [x] and you see [i]Crossed off: beans[/i]. A crossed-off line stays crossed off after you eat or drop the item.
description=[*][b]Finish it.[/b] When every line of a list with three or more items has an [x], you see [i]Got everything on the list![/i] and get 10 less boredom and 5 less unhappiness.
description=[*][b]Bring it home.[/b] If a friend wrote the list, give it back. When they carry the finished list, it gets signed "~ YourName <3". They see [i]YourName brought your groceries![/i] and you see [i]Groceries delivered![/i] You both get 10 less unhappiness.
description=[/list]
description=Each reward pays each person at most once per 24 game hours. The messages still appear during the cooldown. A list pays for its shopping once and for its delivery once.
description=
description=[h2]What counts[/h2]
description=The writer is the first person seen carrying the list with words on it. If you hand over a freshly written list within the same game minute, the receiver counts as the writer. Lists in containers or on the floor do nothing. Items from other mods count by their display name. You can edit a list at any time. The marks come back on the next check.
description=In multiplayer the server does the matching with its own item names, usually English. The delivery bonus needs the shopper to be online when the writer gets the list back.
description=
description=[h2]Setup and multiplayer[/h2]
description=For Project Zomboid [b]Build 42.21[/b]. No required mods. Single player, local split-screen or multiplayer. Install on the server and every client.
description=The server reads the list, keeps the cooldowns and gives the rewards. It saves them across restarts. Verified with a dedicated server and two real clients, including a reconnect. Network split-screen notifications currently target the first local player on each client.
description=
description=[h2]Adding, removing and compatibility[/h2]
description=Safe to add to an existing world. Adds one item and one recipe. No loot spawns. Grocery List items need the mod, so keep it while you still want your lists. Removal leaves inactive cooldown timestamps in world data.
description=The first gallery image is an illustration. Gameplay images come from a disposable demonstration world with two connected players. The home, lighting, clothing, time and starting stats are set for the demo. For the demo the server also handed out the groceries and passed the list between players.
description=
