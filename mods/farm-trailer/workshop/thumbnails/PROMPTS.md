## retro-comic

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: square Steam Workshop thumbnail for the Project Zomboid mod Farm Trailer. Generate one finished 1024x1024 square image, not a sheet of variations.
Primary request: a funny retro illustrated comic-book cover about a towable farm surviving the zombie apocalypse. Bold mid-century pulp/comic screen print, visible halftone, limited golden yellow, tomato red, grass green and black ink, thick confident contours. Full-bleed artwork with a clear outer silhouette.
Scene: a battered pickup truck pulls an ordinary gray metal livestock trailer diagonally across the square. From the trailer's open top rises an absurdly lush tiny vegetable garden and one large contented cow chewing a cabbage. One hapless zombie jogs behind, hopelessly outpaced. The trailer must be visibly wheeled and hitched, not a caravan or a house. Comedic promotional illustration, cheerful defiance.
Composition: huge, clean two-line title occupies the upper third, action fills the lower two thirds. Keep cow, trailer and typography instantly readable when reduced to 128px. Select a few large shapes rather than many tiny details.
Text (verbatim): "FARM" on first line and "TRAILER" on second. Heavy hand-lettered cream comic type with dark outline. No other text.
Avoid: realistic gore, horror close-ups, speech balloons, small print, UI, watermarks, extra words, literal gameplay screenshot, glossy modern app icon.
